RailGo is a railway travel information query tool independently developed by AZ Studio. Our motivation is simple: to make every rail journey calmer and more efficient.

Why RailGo?

As frequent train travelers, we found existing tools either cluttered with information, painfully slow, or drowning in ads. We wanted a pure tool — open it, get accurate results, enjoy a smooth experience.

So RailGo was born. It does not aim to be everything; it focuses on doing "looking up trains" exceptionally well.

Core Principles

  • Minimal: Remove everything unnecessary, let information take center stage.
  • Fast: Local caching, on-demand loading, responses measured in milliseconds.
  • Accurate: Reliable data sources, kept up to date.
  • Free: Core features free forever, no ad interruptions.
info

Notice: RailGo is for informational reference only. For actual ticket booking, please use official channels such as 12306. Train schedules, ticket availability and other information are subject to official releases.
