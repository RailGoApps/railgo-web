Usage Guide

Master all of RailGo's core features in a few minutes.

1. Look Up Train Schedules

Enter a train number (e.g. G1, D2, K3) in the search box on the home screen to view the complete timetable, including:

  • All stops in order
  • Arrival and departure times at each station
  • Dwell time and total duration
  • Train type and operating bureau

2. Check Ticket Availability

Switch to the "Tickets" tab, pick a date and train to view remaining tickets per class in real time. You can also view trends over the coming days.

3. Delay Information

For trains that have departed, RailGo shows the real-time delay status to help you decide whether to adjust connecting plans.

info

Delay data comes from official public APIs and is typically delayed by a few minutes. For reference only.

4. Station-to-Station Search

Enter an origin and destination station and RailGo lists all direct trains, sorted by duration or departure time, so you can pick the best option.

5. Station Details

Tap any station name to open its page, where you can find: basic station info, passing trains, and access guidance.

6. Favorite Frequent Queries

Tap the bookmark icon on a train or station page to add it to your "Favorites" list for quick access next time you open the app.

7. Themes & Settings

RailGo supports Light, Dark, and System themes. In "Settings" you can switch themes, adjust font size, and manage cache.
