Privacy Policy

updateLast updated: 2024 privacy_tipAZ Studio

We understand how important your personal information is. This policy explains how RailGo collects, uses, and protects your data. Please read it carefully.

lock

Our promise: RailGo collects as little as possible and never sells your personal data.

1. Information We Collect

RailGo follows data minimization. We may collect:

  • Query records: processed temporarily to serve queries, not stored long-term on servers.
  • Device info: model, OS version, app version, for troubleshooting.
  • Usage stats: anonymous feature usage frequency, for product improvement.
  • Favorites data: stored on your local device, not uploaded.

2. How We Use Information

  • Provide and improve the railway info service;
  • Diagnose and fix bugs;
  • Analyze usage patterns to optimize experience;
  • Contact you when necessary (e.g. if you proactively reach out).

3. Data Storage

Your favorites and settings are stored on your local device. Query data is discarded after processing and not stored long-term on servers.

4. Third-Party Services

RailGo's data comes from public railway info APIs. We may use:

  • Railway data APIs (for query features);
  • Crash reporting (anonymous, only on errors);
  • Analytics (aggregated anonymous data).

Each has its own privacy policy — we recommend reading them.

5. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and administrative measures to protect your data. However, no internet transmission is absolutely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Your Rights

  • Access & correct: view and edit locally stored data anytime.
  • Delete: uninstall the app or clear data to remove all local info.
  • Withdraw consent: stop using the relevant features.

7. Minors

RailGo is not directed at minors and does not knowingly collect their information. If we discover such collection by mistake, please contact us to delete it.

8. Policy Changes

We may update this policy. Significant changes will be announced via in-app notice.

9. Contact

For questions about this policy, contact us via Report & Feedback.
