warning

Beware of counterfeits

The app is entirely free to use. Recently we found third-party sites tampering with the program — the downloaded RailGo installer pops up payment windows and shows QR codes to lure you into paying after installation. These are all fakes — do not transfer money. We have reported all such violating sites and had their resources taken down.

Users are also prohibited from uploading exe installers or related resources to third-party sites, or reselling them privately. Please comply consciously and obtain the software only from official channels — stay alert against scams.

How to Report

If you encounter websites or accounts that impersonate, resell for payment, or fake RailGo software and data services, please contact 心碎上海南 (Xin Sui Shang Hai Nan) for fast escalation to the authorities.

Please stay alert to the recent surge of AI-based impersonation. Verified reports will be rewarded.
