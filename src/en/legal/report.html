Report & Feedback

Found a bug? Have a suggestion? Let us know — every piece of feedback makes RailGo better.

Channels

Reach us through any of the following:

mail
Email railgo@azstudio.dev
forum
QQ Group Join the community
bug_report
GitHub Issue Submit a bug or feature request

Please Include

  • Device info: model, OS version
  • RailGo version: found in "Settings → About"
  • Reproduction steps: the more detailed the better
  • Screenshots or recordings: if available
warning

Reports should relate to the RailGo app itself. Railway operations complaints should go to 12306 or the relevant railway bureau.

Response Time

We aim to process feedback promptly. General issues are answered within 3 business days; complex ones may take longer — thanks for your patience.
